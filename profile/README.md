# Anticipation Consulting

Boutique data science and AI consultancy led by [Brian C. Keegan, Ph.D.](https://www.anticipationconsulting.com/about/) — computational social scientist and Associate Professor at CU Boulder. Work spans predictive, causal, and network modeling; algorithm and data audits; and expert analysis in high-stakes settings.

[anticipationconsulting.com](https://www.anticipationconsulting.com) · Boulder, Colorado

- Predictive, causal, network, and language-model analysis
- Data and algorithm audits for bias, leakage, and error
- Research design and mixed-methods community studies
- Expert reports, depositions, and trial testimony
- Data strategy, storytelling, and team training
- Open-source intelligence and public data collection

Online platforms and social media · Trust, safety, and misinformation · Network and infrastructure analysis · Civic and public-sector data · Crisis and high-tempo events · Algorithmic accountability and regulation
