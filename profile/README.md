## About

**[Brian C. Keegan, Ph.D.](https://www.anticipationconsulting.com/about/)** is a computational social scientist and Associate Professor at the University of Colorado Boulder. Over 15 years of applied and academic research, his work has examined how data, algorithms, and people behave under pressure — in newsrooms, on social platforms, across civic institutions, and inside organizations navigating high-stakes decisions.

His credentials span MIT, Northwestern, Northeastern, and Harvard Business School. He has provided expert analysis in regulatory proceedings, litigation, and policy contexts where the quality of evidence is not negotiable.
